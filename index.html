Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?

Next Chapter Woman is for women aged 40–65 who are redesigning the second half of life — mainly in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Whether you are working, returning to work, building something new, or simply ready for a more intentional next chapter, you are welcome here.

Is this medical or financial advice?

No. Everything in Next Chapter Woman is education only. The Health Hub covers general healthy-aging education, and the Wealth Hub covers general money and retirement education. For personal decisions, please talk to your doctor and to a qualified fiduciary adviser who knows your situation.

What do I get as a Founding Member?

You get the 40+ Reinvention Blueprint, access to all four hubs as they open through the founding season, the weekly Next Chapter Letter, and founding-member community access including accountability circles. Your $19/month price is locked for as long as your membership stays active — the regular price will be $39/month.

How does joining work at launch?

Membership is handled personally at launch. Tap “Become a Founding Member” to get in touch, and your welcome pack will arrive by email within 24 hours with your next steps.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime. There is no long-term contract — stay for as long as the membership is useful to you.

Do I need to be “tech-savvy” or have a business already?

Not at all. The Blueprint and the hubs are written in plain language and broken into small steps. The Wealth Hub starts with ideas and confidence, whether you have never earned online before or you are returning after a break.

Please read — an honest note

Next Chapter Woman provides educational content only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Always talk to your doctor about health decisions and to a qualified fiduciary adviser about money decisions that affect you personally. Everyone’s health, finances, experience and circumstances are different, so results vary from person to person. We do not guarantee any income, health outcome or specific result — the ideas here are starting points for learning and planning, not promises.
